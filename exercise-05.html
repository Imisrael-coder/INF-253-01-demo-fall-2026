What's my goal?

My Career Goals

My goal is to become a data analyst one day. I am passionate about data—understanding it, grasping its trends, and interpreting it. I do not simply want to practice this profession within a specific company; I want to work independently and start my own business. My aim is not to focus only on improving corporate performance by helping companies make informed decisions, but rather to understand society as a whole—specifically how technological advancements, such as AI, influence or transform our ways of life.

Some of the skills I want to develop include:

  • Data analysis
  • Data visualization
  • Critical thinking
  • Understanding trends
  • Using AI and technology
